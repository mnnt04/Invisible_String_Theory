A PERSONAL ART EXHIBITION

Behind the Strings

Curatorial Statement

I first came across Invisible String Theory on TikTok. It's the idea that people, places and experiences are connected in ways we might not even realize. As I've grown older, I've found myself noticing these connections more often.

For this exhibition, I selected five artworks from the Getty Museum and Rijksmuseum collections that reminded me of my own experiences. Some connections are tied to places I've visited, while others remind me of my family, childhood and cultural background.

What interests me most is the possibility of connections I may never be able to prove.

I wanted to use these artworks to explore how something created long before I was born could still feel connected to my life. The red strings represent these connections, while the photograph of me as a baby represents where my own story begins.
